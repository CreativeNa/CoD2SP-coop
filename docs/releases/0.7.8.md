## What changed since 0.7.7

- Partner's pistol poses: standing and crouching idle, crouch run, standing and crouching reload and the shot clips of the game's own soldiers (a second body of the partner with its own animation tree).
- A failed mission is shared: when the mission fails for one player (a protected ally is hurt, an objective is lost, friendly fire) the other player sees the game's own fail screen and both return to the shared checkpoint. The partner is not killed.
- Enemy tanks and 88 mm guns that one player destroys are destroyed for the other player too (`88ridge`, `libya`; needs `seta coop_keykill "on"` on both PCs).
- The partner's tank copy rocks and is pushed back when he fires, has the game's own engine sound (idle / moving) and raises dust behind its tracks while it moves.
- Placed machine gun, experimental (first version): when the partner operates a placed MG42, his body takes the gunner pose and a copy of the gun stands in the place of the map gun and turns to where he aims; the real gun is out of sight meanwhile.
- Fixed: continuous fire of the partner was shown in parts with pauses (rounds were counted per script pass; a burst longer than eight rounds counted as one; the partner's bullets were switched off whenever his line of fire passed within 130 units of you, now 60 for a machine gun).
- Experimental: `coop_enemysync` (on by default, `seta coop_enemysync "off"` switches it off): a trigger received from the partner that spawns enemies only is fired at once, wherever you are. Triggers that spawn allies keep the old rule (they wait while you are more than 1500 units from the partner). With `coop_debug 1`, `coop5.log` shows why a held trigger did not qualify and the number of living enemies and allies every 10 seconds (`COOPEN`).
- Only this PC and the linked partner may send to the game's UDP port 27201 (before, any address could, including the test commands).
- Checkpoint lines in `coop5.log` carry the CRC-32 of the file (sent and received); `tools/compare_logs.py` compares the `coop5.log` of both PCs and says what each PC sent and what became of it on the other. By default it reads only the last game start of each file; `--all` reads everything.
- The installer checks the animation names of the mod against the engine limit while it builds.
- Documentation: a security note (docs/SECURITY.md): who to play with, ports and firewall, logs, reporting a problem.
