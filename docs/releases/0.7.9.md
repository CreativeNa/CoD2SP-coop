## What changed since 0.7.8

- The installer no longer stops on a build of `CoD2SP_s.exe` that it does not know: it prints a notice and goes on. The mod was made and tested on the Steam build and on the retail 1.3 build only; on another version or edition the game may not start or the co-op may not connect. In that case choose **Uninstall** and send `coop5.log`.
- Mission order: with a linked partner the next mission after a finished one is the next one in the campaign order on both PCs (it was chosen from each PC's own profile, so missions could be skipped or played again).
- The destruction of map-placed flak guns, tanks and halftracks of more missions is mirrored to the partner (for example the two flak guns of `decoytown`: the binocular artillery of that mission is enabled only after they are destroyed; needs `seta coop_keykill "on"` on both PCs).
- A checkpoint load that was scheduled after the partner's death is dropped when the mission has changed meanwhile.
